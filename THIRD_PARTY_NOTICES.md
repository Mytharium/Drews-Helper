Third-Party Notices
===================

Drew's Helper includes `src/main/resources/collision-map.zip` from Runemoro's
`shortest-path` project as the walking-collision data source for waypoint route
guidance.

Drew's Helper also includes `src/main/resources/drewshelper-transports.tsv`,
generated by C2 from selected transport TSV resources in Skretzo's maintained
`shortest-path` fork. The generated Drew file keeps baseline click/pay transport
edges and a Wilderness lever/obelisk category used by Drew's own route graph.
